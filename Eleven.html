Eleven: Bohemian Pilsner

Eleven is an award-winning Bohemian style Pilsner. It won GOLD in the 2024 New York State Beer Competition.

Tasting Notes:

Herbal and subtly peppery hops balance with a deceptively complex malt backbone for our take on a traditional Czech lager.
