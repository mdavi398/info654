Strong Rope's Best Beers

Wolf Sweater: Hazy IPA

Wolf Sweater is a hazy IPA, a style popularized in the United States Northeast region, often referred to as a "New England IPA." It is sadly discontinued. Try Wolf in the Garden instead!

Tasting Notes:

Hazy and juicy notes of fresh pineapple, melon, and cantaloupe on a soft malt base.
