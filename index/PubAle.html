Strong Rope's Best Beers

Pub Ale: Extra Special Bitter

Pub Ale is a classic Strong Rope beer, and loved by many. It is a flagship beer and usually available in cans and on draft.

Tasting Notes:

Floral hop with notes of lemon rind and fresh pine needle on pale, bready malt, satisfying bitter finish.
